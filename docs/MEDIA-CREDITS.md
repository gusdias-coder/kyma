## Type and identity

Inter and Oswald were obtained from Google Fonts, subset to Latin and converted to WOFF2. Internal subset names were changed to KYMA UI and KYMA Display. Original font licenses are included as [INTER-OFL.txt](INTER-OFL.txt) and [OSWALD-OFL.txt](OSWALD-OFL.txt). CSS family aliases do not change the underlying license.

The SVG KYMA wordmark, symbol, favicon and line icons were created for this concept. No third-party brand identity was copied.
